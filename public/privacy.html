Privacy

What Orbit stores, where it lives, and how to remove it.

If you use Orbit as a guest

Your profile, skill ratings, exam results, roadmap progress, notes and project links stay in this browser (local storage). They are not sent to the server. "Erase all data" in Settings removes them.

If you sign in

  • We store your email address, name, a one-way hash of your password, and the same progress data as above.
  • Email, name and progress are encrypted in the database. Passwords are never stored, only a salted scrypt hash.
  • For each signed-in device we keep a random session token (stored hashed), the browser name, and when it was last used, so you can sign devices out in Settings.
  • Your email is used only to confirm your address and to reset your password. We do not send marketing email.
  • One cookie keeps you signed in (HttpOnly, Secure on https). Your theme choice is saved in local storage. There is no advertising or analytics tracking.

AI coaching

AI features are optional and only work when you are signed in and the server has an AI key. When you use them, the server sends Claude your study field, year, selected interests, skill ratings, skill gaps, career matches, progress and your question. It does not send your name or email. Without AI, coaching text is rule-based and nothing leaves the server.

Delete or export

Settings lets you download your data as JSON, sign out everywhere, and delete your account. Deleting an account removes the profile, progress and sessions from the database.

Orbit is a student project. If you run your own copy for real users, add your contact details here and have the wording reviewed for the rules that apply to you.
